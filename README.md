# Pathways

Know what your education can lead to before you choose it.

This is the published website. It is built by `scripts/mvp_export.py` in the project files
(`site/index.html` plus `site/data/*.json`) and copied here. Every figure comes from public sources:
the U.S. Department of Education (College Scorecard, IPEDS), the U.S. Census Bureau (PSEO),
O*NET, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and each school's own published graduate outcomes report.
